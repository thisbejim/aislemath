A FAIR COMPARISON

Sticker price is only half the story.

Unit price puts every shelf label on the same ruler. ShelfWise also handles the promotions that make ordinary mental math unreliable.

01

Normalize the size

12 oz, 500 g, and 1 lb can be compared together. We convert them to a common base unit; weight, volume, and count stay separate because they measure different things.

02

Apply the real deal

“2 for $5”, buy-one-get-one, percentage discounts, and coupons are translated into an effective price per pack before ranking.

03

Show the trade-off

See what you would spend for the number of packs you need, how much the winner saves, and whether the bigger box is actually the better buy.

THE MATH, VISIBLE

Effective unit price = deal-adjusted pack price ÷ normalized package size

There is no hidden recommendation engine or price feed. You provide the shelf facts; ShelfWise shows the arithmetic and keeps your list in this browser.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions people ask at the shelf

Does ShelfWise send my prices anywhere?

No. Calculations happen in this tab. A draft is saved only in your browser’s local storage so it can still be here when you return. A share link contains your entered comparison only when you explicitly copy it.

Can I compare grams with ounces?

Yes. Weight units are converted to grams, and volume units to millilitres. ShelfWise will not compare a weight item with a volume item because that would require guessing a product’s density.

How should I enter a multi-buy?

Enter the ordinary shelf price, choose “Multi-buy”, then enter the number of packs and the advertised deal price. For example, “2 for $5” means deal packs 2 and deal price 5.

Is the cheapest unit price always the best choice?

Not necessarily. Shelf life, storage space, quality, and what you can afford today matter too. The tool gives you a transparent price comparison, not a command to buy more.
